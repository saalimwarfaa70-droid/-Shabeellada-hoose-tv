JAAMACADA CAALAMIGA AH EE BARAAWA AYAA FURTAY IS DIIWAAN GELINTA ARDADA SANAD DUGSIYEEDKA 2026/27

Jaamacada Caalamiga ah ee Baraawa ayaa rasmi uga dhawaaqday furitaanka is-diiwaangelinta ardayda cusub ee sanad dugsiyeedka 2026/27, iyada oo ugu baaqday ardayda dooneysa inay ku biiraan kuliyadaha kala duwan ee jaamacadda inay soo buuxiyaan shuruudaha looga baahan yahay.
